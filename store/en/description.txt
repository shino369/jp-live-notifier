Never miss a new live of your oshi.

Oshi Live Alert watches Japan's major ticket sites for the artists you follow and tells you as soon as a new live or concert is listed. Add your artists once; the extension checks for you in the background, from every 30 minutes to every 12 hours (default: every hour).

TICKET SITES
• Works with Japan's major ticket sites, such as e+ and ローチケ, and with streaming-event sites. Settings lists every supported site, each with its own switch and status.
• Four of them start switched off because their terms of use forbid automated access. Settings marks them and links their terms; turning one on is your choice and your risk.

FEATURES
• One inbox for every site: the same show on several sites becomes one item, with a ticket row per site and sale (先行, 一般発売, …).
• Quiet first sync: when you add an artist, what is already listed goes into the inbox unread, with one summary notification instead of dozens.
• Smart matching: a listing matches when its title or performers contain the artist's name; full/half width, upper/lower case, and hiragana/katakana are ignored. Past shows are dropped.
• System notifications for new shows, and an unread count on the toolbar icon.
• Email-style inbox: search, unread and starred filters, keyboard shortcuts, star to keep, "clear read" to tidy up. A deleted show does not come back.
• Light and dark themes, following your system. English and Japanese.
• Polite to the sites: one request per site at a time, at least 3 seconds apart. A site that blocks or fails waits before it is checked again.
• Optional, off by default: with "Get past bot checks in a background tab" on, a site that answers with a bot check is opened once in an inactive tab; the extension reads the search results there and closes the tab.

PRIVACY
No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Your artists, inbox, and settings stay in your browser. The extension sends your artists' names only to the ticket sites you have turned on, as search words, the same as when you search there yourself.
